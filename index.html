Rules & Multipliers

Over7Under Game Rules

Our online browser game adheres to authentic, transparent dice rules. The game uses two fair standard dice with outcomes totaling anywhere from 2 to 12. Here is exactly how rounds, predictions, and payouts work:

Higher

Guess Higher (Over 7)

Winning condition: Combined dice sum is 8, 9, 10, 11, or 12.

Payout: 2x your bet (100% profit). For example, a 300 bet returns 600 credits.

Push rule on 7: If the dice land on exactly 7, your 300 bet is safely refunded.

Lower

Guess Lower (Under 7)

Winning condition: Combined dice sum is 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6.

Payout: 2x your bet (100% profit). For example, a 300 bet returns 600 credits.

Push rule on 7: If the dice land on exactly 7, your 300 bet is safely refunded.

Exactly 7

Guess Exactly 7

Winning condition: Combined dice sum is exactly 7.

Payout: 4x your bet (300% profit). For example, a 300 bet returns 1,200 credits!

Loss condition: Any roll other than 7 loses the wagered bet.

Why the 7 Push Rule Matters to Players

In standard dice mathematics, 7 is the single most common sum. By including a Push rule on Higher and Lower predictions, whenever 7 appears, you don't lose your stake—it returns straight back into your bankroll. This eliminates house advantage and creates a remarkably fair casual dynamic.
